## Design Context

### Users
Primary users are patients aged 25-55 who are evaluating dermatology and aesthetic treatments in Belgrade. They typically visit on mobile first, compare credibility quickly, and want confidence before contacting the clinic.

### Brand Personality
Calm, precise, premium. The interface should feel medically trustworthy, emotionally reassuring, and quietly high-end rather than flashy.

### Aesthetic Direction
Maintain the current homepage direction and refine it toward clean editorial luxury: warm neutrals, disciplined red accents, generous whitespace, and subtle depth. Visual tone should stay modern and polished with restrained motion and clear content hierarchy.

### Design Principles
1. Trust first: every section should reinforce expertise, care, and credibility.
2. Quiet premium: use subtle contrast, spacing rhythm, and refined surfaces over decorative effects.
3. Action clarity: key paths to booking and services must be obvious without visual noise.
4. Consistency at scale: typography, spacing, and interaction states should feel systemized across sections.
5. Mobile confidence: layouts must preserve readability, tap comfort, and visual calm on small screens.
